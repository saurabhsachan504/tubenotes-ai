What TubeNotes does

TubeNotes helps people turn publicly available YouTube videos into structured summaries, key points, detailed notes, translations and print-ready PDF documents. It is built for learners, professionals and anyone who wants to review video information more efficiently.

Understand fasterTurn long videos into clear, structured takeaways.

Keep learning practicalSave detailed notes as a PDF for later review and study.

How the service works

You provide a supported YouTube URL. TubeNotes retrieves available video information and captions, where accessible, and uses AI models to produce the requested output. The generated result is intended to help with review and understanding; it is not a substitute for watching the source video or independently verifying important information.

Our approach

  • Clarity: useful output should be easy to scan and act on.
  • Respect for users: account, device and payment controls are designed to protect the service and its community.
  • Responsible AI: generated content may contain omissions or errors, so users should check the original source for important decisions.
